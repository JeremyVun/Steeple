Privacy policy

How Steeple handles your information. This is a plain-English summary for our early preview — we'll expand it before any public launch or app-store release.

Version 2026-09-20

What we collect

You can browse everything on Steeple without an account, and we set no cookie to let you. We do record, in aggregate, how the listings are browsed — which spaces are looked at, which searches find nothing — so that we can make the map more useful. Those records are tied to a random id for the visit, not to you.

Your account

If you sign in, you do it with Google or Apple — we never see or store a password. From your sign-in we keep your name, your email address (Apple lets you hide it behind a relay address), and the fact that your account came from that provider. We use these to run your account and to let venues know who's asking about their space, nothing else.

You can delete your account from your account page at any time — your name and email are removed from your account immediately, and you're signed out everywhere. Booking records, messages, ratings and agreement records may remain under our retention rules. Deleting an account does not erase information already shared with a venue or copies held in backups.

Asking for a space

A request you send, and everything either of you says about it afterwards, is held at Steeple so that both of you can read it back. The venue sees your name, the group you named, what you asked for and when, and whatever you wrote. We are notified of the same, because it is our service the correspondence lives on.

Paying for a space

For bookings paid directly to the venue, Steeple does not collect your payment details. Arrange payment with the host using the method you agree with them. Hosts who choose to set up a payment account provide onboarding information to Stripe; Steeple stores the account identifier and its setup status, not the host's bank account details.

Cookies

One, and it is essential: if you are signed in, we set a first-party cookie holding the token that keeps you signed in. Your browser will not let any script read it. We don't use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Your choices & contact

You can browse without signing in or providing personal details. For any privacy question, or to ask us to remove information, get in touch with us.

This is a preview policy and will be replaced with a full, dated privacy policy before launch.
